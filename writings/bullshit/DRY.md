Don’t repeat years.

Years of predictable errors before you. Years of wasted effort. Years of churning different things into a single pit, and same things out of it.

Don’t repeat making the right decision.

Each specific right decision can be made once and that is enough. If it is right, don’t make a different right decision on the same line. Leave it a sole point.

But repeat pointers to the right decision. From everywhere, there should be a chain up to it. Always repeat this process and never stop.

Also, repeat yourself loud and clear, each time the right decision was ignored - repeat yourself. Each time this happens - repeat it happening in the chain of pointers. Scale down the probability of this happening.

Repeat orthogonal decisions, even if they are both equally right and spelled the same. When the time changes, and this point on orthogonal lines diverges into different paths - you will thank yourself for it. And because you left pointers everywhere to these decisions, anybody can follow down each of these paths.

Always repeat the mold of the right decision. Once you have it - you know it. Take it and repeatedly use it all the time, until it doesn’t fit. Then - an orthogonal one. Molds were also secretly decisions all along. There is never a better mold than one that already produces the right decision on this line. Don’t overthink it too much.
